Four directions for the landing page

The landing page will be the front door to our energy and data center siting maps. Each option below shows the same content and coverage in a different look and motion. Open each one, try it on a desktop and a phone, and note which direction you would carry forward.

  1. A

    Control room with energize

    The page as a grid operator's board. Each state is a lamp; covered states light up as gold power reaches them along the bus lines.

    Open option A
  2. B

    Night lights with energize

    The country as seen from orbit at night. Gold trails carry power west to east and each covered state switches on as they arrive.

    Open option B
  3. C

    Isometric campus with story

    Scroll slowly: the campus assembles from power plant to transmission, substation, feeder and data hall, then pulls back to the national board.

    Open option C
  4. D

    Isometric campus with energize

    The same drawings as C, but the first screen comes alive on load: current runs to the data hall and covered states rise on the board.

    Open option D

What is the same everywhere

Headline, copy, coverage data and behavior. Choosing a state opens its details; the directory lists every map.

Map links are hidden

On this review site, maps show "Access by invitation". Once the apps are gated, each one opens to its own sign-in page.

Motion settings

If your device is set to reduce motion, every option shows a complete still version instead.
