THESIS
Decennial Energy Maps is the United States at night: covered states are lit fields of light, everything else is a faint grid of unlit land, and gold is current in motion. A visitor sees where the maps reach before reading a word.

OWN-WORLD
Satellite "Earth at night" imagery rendered as a screen-fixed dot matrix on an ink-derived night ground (#1b1420). Hosting capacity is lilac light, parcel-level siting the brightest and densest wash light, partial a dotted hatch, unlit land one dot in four. Gold #EBBE07 is live power only: corridors, pulses, the selected state, focus. One bounded blur makes area haze, never a neon edge. No invented points: light fills state shapes or travels along the core corridors.

STORY
Power path at night: one gold plant light, a line of transmission tower lights with a gold trail, a substation grid, finer feeder dots, the data hall's dense white field; then the camera pulls back to the national night field. Energize: dusk to night as gold trails reach each state and its lights strike on. Zoom: the matrix tightens as the camera moves from the national transmission view to the Northeast feeders. Micro: filters put one tier's lights out and relight the other.

FIRST VIEWPORT
At 1440x900 the hero is the night map at full width; the headline "Megawatts before acres." is set inside the map's dark, uncovered interior over the northern plains, with the subhead and the working Find your state control over the Southwest, the CA to OH corridor between them. The lit coasts and the Great Lakes to Mid-Atlantic cluster frame the copy; the south of the map meets the fold.
