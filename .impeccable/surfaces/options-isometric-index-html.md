THESIS: Decennial Energy Maps presented as an engineering drawing set. One isometric camera shows a generic data center campus fed from high voltage, and beneath it the national hex board on a plinth whose covered tiles stand up from the ground. Megawatts before acres: the power path is drawn first, the land second.

OWN-WORLD: Isometric development renderings. A light drawing sheet with a framed border, a ruled title block, aubergine linework at one stroke weight, faces shaded top white, left lilac, right lilac shade, gold only for live conductors and flowing current. Lattice towers, dead-end gantry, transformers with radiator fins, bus structure, feeder poles, a long low data hall with rooftop units and a generator yard. The directory is an equipment schedule; the legend is a key of extruded tiles.

STORY: The visitor reads the headline in the title block, picks a state in the find field, and sees coverage on the board at the fold. Scrolling, they learn how power reaches a data hall (generation, transmission, substation, feeder, hall) and that the maps read the grid at both scales, then land on the working board, the covered-state index and the schedule of every map, each one click from its app.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header with the DECENNIAL wordmark and two anchors. A drawing sheet: the headline large and light at top left, the title block below it holding the subhead and the working find control, the campus plate at right with HV lines entering from the sheet edge, and the extruded hex board rising at the bottom edge, clickable.
