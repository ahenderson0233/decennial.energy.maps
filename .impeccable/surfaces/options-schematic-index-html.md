THESIS
Decennial Energy Maps is the one-line diagram of where data centers can take power. The page is drawn the way every interconnection study is drawn: a heavy bus, breakers, transformers and feeders on a white sheet, with gold marking what is energized. Megawatts before acres reads as engineering, not marketing.

OWN-WORLD
The electrical single-line diagram with ANSI/IEEE device conventions. Ground: a white drawing sheet inside an aubergine frame on a wash surround. Material: aubergine linework in a strict weight hierarchy (7px bus, 4px transmission, 2.5px feeder, 2px devices, 1px annotation); gold #EBBE07 only as the energized core of conductors and the fill of closed breakers; deepened lilac fills for hosting capacity. Type: Work Sans, set like drawing annotation for labels and as a heavy display for the headline. The main bus is the page spine; every section is a feeder tapped through a breaker and its heading is the feeder label; the footer is the title block, the bus ends in a ground.

STORY
A visitor lands on the sheet, sees the headline as the first feeder off the bus and the key plan of covered states energized by that feeder, and can pick a state at once. Scrolling follows the power path (generation, transmission, substation, feeder, data hall) or the two load scales, then reaches the main drawing: the geographic coverage map, the covered-state list and the switchboard of every map.

FIRST VIEWPORT
At 1440x900: generator and wordmark at the top of the bus; the hero feeder leaving the bus through a breaker and running over "Megawatts before acres." to a transformer, then into a clickable geographic key plan; subhead and the working Find your state control under the headline; the next feeder's tap visible at the fold. At 390x844 the key plan sits at the fold under the find control, fed straight from the bus.
