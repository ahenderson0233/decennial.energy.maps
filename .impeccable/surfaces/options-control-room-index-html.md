THESIS
The landing page is a grid operator's control-room wall board: lit lamps are covered states, dark glass is honest absence, and gold is current. A visitor reads coverage the way an operator reads a board, at a glance, and presses a lamp to open the right map.

OWN-WORLD
Mosaic-tile board face in ink, aubergine panels with seams and engraved sentence-case labels, lamp lenses in bezels with a recess, a hex mimic board, an annunciator panel of engraved windows, illuminated pushbuttons and a two-position rotary selector switch. Material carries the world; nothing glows.

STORY
Generation to data hall is drawn as a mimic diagram that scroll energizes station by station, then the board zooms out to the national hex board lighting west to east. Energize sends pulses down straight bus lines that light each covered lamp. Zoom turns a selector switch that re-arms lamps between transmission and distribution. Micro arms lamps by tier with pushbuttons.

FIRST VIEWPORT
Wordmark rail, a left-set display headline "Megawatts before acres.", the subhead, the working Find your state select, and a full-width annunciator panel of the covered states as lit, pressable windows: the living part of the board, routing in one press.
